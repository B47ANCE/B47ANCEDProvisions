description=[h2]Current Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Dry 22 whole vanilla fruit types at native small-plant drying racks. Results retain the original item ID, type, calories and macronutrients, with carried age, quarter weight and no thirst benefit.
description=[*]Make jam or strained jelly from Apple, Blackberry, Blueberry, Cherry, Grapes, Peach or Strawberry, plus an additional apple for pectin, eight whole SugarCubes items, an empty jar and pristine lid.
description=[*]Use an empty pot and kitchen knife beside a powered, switched-on stove at 100 C or above. Jelly also needs a strainer and cheesecloth. Microwaves are excluded.
description=[*]Jelly defaults to 70% of fruit food values plus all sugar; remaining fruit values return as edible pulp. Open sealed jars from inventory before eating. Opening returns a lid; normal food use returns an empty jar when finished.
description=[*]Server validation, native timed actions, food aging and inventory synchronization; preserved nutrition and relative age.
description=[*]Read-only food-state inspection and the existing ingredient/appliance/preservation API foundations.
description=[*]Transparent subject-only mod icon; original project thumbnails retained.
description=[/list]

description=[h2]Compatibility and Requirements[/h2]
description=
description=Installed-source audit: Project Zomboid Build 42.21, Steam build 25485521. Native SP and dedicated multiplayer NOT TESTED. G7 and stable-release G8 remain BLOCKED. No required mods or custom Java/JAR dependencies. Existing foodWorks Lua/API, sandbox and persistence identifiers remain compatible. Whole/default unmodified ingredients in main inventory are required; poisoned, stale, frozen, partial, filled or unsuitable inputs are rejected. Foraged instances with different nutrition/metadata may be rejected.

description=[h2]Configuration[/h2]
description=
description=Server sandbox settings control fruit drying (enabled by default), drying hours (48), dried shelf-life multiplier (6), jams/jellies (enabled), sealed fresh days (30), action time multiplier (1) and jelly fruit yield (70%). Existing batches/jars retain their accepted food settings. Food inspection and the read-only apple/rack preview have separate toggles.
