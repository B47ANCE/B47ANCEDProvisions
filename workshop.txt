description=[h2]Current Features[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Vanilla and third-party food adapter registry
description=[*]Normalized ingredient, food-property, freshness, and identity inspection
description=[*]Read-only packaging, taint, freezing/thawing, aging, rotten-replacement, and compost-state inspection
description=[*]Optional inventory action showing normalized food, preservation, effect, and nutrition state without changing the item
description=[*]Recoverable appliance-process transaction contract for exact power and water reservations
description=[*]Persistent named-preservation jobs with protected inputs, cadence, spoilage, cancellation, and recovery
description=[*]Stable identities for cross-mod processing and recipes
description=[*]Evidence-audited foundations for quality, preservation, and contamination semantics
description=[*]Provider isolation and nil-safe extension points
description=[*]Default-on sandbox control for the food inspection action
