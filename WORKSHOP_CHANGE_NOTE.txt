Seven vanilla fruit choices with an additional apple for pectin, whole SugarCubes, an empty jar/pristine lid and tools. Use a hot powered stove through vanilla server timed actions. [b]Version 0.8.0-dev.3[/b]

[h2]Vanilla fruit-drying expansion[/h2]

Extends server-owned in-place drying to 22 vanilla fruit types. Every batch retains its exact original type/ID and nutrients, carries age and uses saved server settings. Existing Apple v1 batches remain supported; non-Apple jobs require this version. Original fruit visuals remain.

Adopts the 149-item vanilla preservation catalog. Jam, jelly, marmalade, fruit butter, pickling, curing and fermentation are not implemented: safe multi-input nutrition and heated/sealed-holder contracts remain blocked.

[b]Version 0.8.0-dev.2[/b]

[h2]Server-owned in-place apple drying[/h2]

Dry one whole fresh apple in a native small-plant rack, check progress, cancel or collect. The same Base.Apple item becomes marked dried apple slices: unchanged calories/macros/hunger, carried age and scaled freshness thresholds, quarter weight and no thirst benefit.

Server sandbox defaults are 48 observed loaded game hours and 6x shelf life. Unloaded racks pause progress. Exact item/actor/access validation, a private server ledger, separate item snapshots, carried-batch recovery and post-conversion resource synchronization are implemented.

MP testing build - dedicated multiplayer NOT TESTED. Native SP NOT TESTED; G7 and stable G8 BLOCKED. Base.Apple identity is retained; no distinct dried-food type or native recipe. Offline testing only; no publication is implied.
