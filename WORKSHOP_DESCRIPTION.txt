[h1]B47ANCE’d Provisions[/h1]

Cooking, food preparation, ingredients, freshness, and food-system infrastructure for theWorks ecosystem.

B47ANCE’d Provisions provides normalized food identity and properties that future recipes, preservation systems, and compatible mods can share without replacing vanilla behavior prematurely.

[b]Mod Version: 0.8.0-dev.4[/b]

[b]Development Status[/b]

MP testing build - dedicated multiplayer NOT TESTED

Development build with in-place drying for 22 vanilla fruit types plus apple-assisted jams and strained jellies for seven fruit choices. Offline checks pass; native SP/dedicated behavior remains untested. This is not a stable release.

Jams and jellies use fresh vanilla fruit, an additional apple, whole sugar cubes, an empty jar/lid and tools at a hot powered stove. Jelly returns edible strained pulp. Open sealed jars before eating. Marmalade, fruit butter, pickling, curing and fermentation remain unimplemented.

[h2]Current Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Vanilla and third-party food adapter registry
[*]Normalized ingredient, food-property, freshness, and identity inspection
[*]Read-only packaging, taint, freezing/thawing, aging, rotten-replacement, and compost-state inspection
[*]Optional inventory action showing normalized food, preservation, effect, and nutrition state without changing the item
[*]Server-owned drying for 22 vanilla fruit types at native small-plant racks; preserves each original item ID/type and nutrients, carries age, reduces weight and removes thirst benefit
[*]Server-owned jam/jelly actions, sealed/opened jars, edible pulp and carried relative age; vanilla tools, stove heat, inventory synchronization and jam visuals
[*]Server sandbox preserve availability, processing time, sealed shelf life and jelly yield
[*]Server sandbox drying hours and shelf-life multiplier; existing batches keep accepted settings; unloaded racks pause progress
[*]Read-only apple/rack drying prechecks with localized unknown/failed checks; no processing or eligibility authorization
[*]Recoverable appliance-process transaction contract for exact power and water reservations
[*]Persistent named-preservation jobs with protected inputs, cadence, spoilage, cancellation, and recovery
[*]Stable identities for cross-mod processing and recipes
[*]Evidence-audited foundations for quality, preservation, and contamination semantics
[*]Provider isolation and nil-safe extension points
[*]Default-on sandbox control for the food inspection action
[/list]
