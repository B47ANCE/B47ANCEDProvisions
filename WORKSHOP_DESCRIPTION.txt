[h1]B47ANCE’d Provisions[/h1]

Lua-only fruit preservation using vanilla food, inventory, stove and drying-rack systems.

[b]Mod Version: 0.8.0-dev.5[/b]

MP testing build - dedicated multiplayer NOT TESTED

[h2]Current Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Dry 22 whole vanilla fruit types at native small-plant drying racks. Results retain the original item ID, type, calories and macronutrients, with carried age, quarter weight and no thirst benefit.
[*]Make jam or strained jelly from Apple, Blackberry, Blueberry, Cherry, Grapes, Peach or Strawberry, plus an additional apple for pectin, eight whole SugarCubes items, an empty jar and pristine lid.
[*]Use an empty pot and kitchen knife beside a powered, switched-on stove at 100 C or above. Jelly also needs a strainer and cheesecloth. Microwaves are excluded.
[*]Jelly defaults to 70% of fruit food values plus all sugar; remaining fruit values return as edible pulp. Open sealed jars from inventory before eating. Opening returns a lid; normal food use returns an empty jar when finished.
[*]Server validation, native timed actions, food aging and inventory synchronization; preserved nutrition and relative age.
[*]Read-only food-state inspection and the existing ingredient/appliance/preservation API foundations.
[*]Transparent subject-only mod icon; original project thumbnails retained.
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility and Requirements[/h2]

Installed-source audit: Project Zomboid Build 42.21, Steam build 25485521. Native SP and dedicated multiplayer NOT TESTED. G7 and stable-release G8 remain BLOCKED. No required mods or custom Java/JAR dependencies. Existing foodWorks Lua/API, sandbox and persistence identifiers remain compatible. Whole/default unmodified ingredients in main inventory are required; poisoned, stale, frozen, partial, filled or unsuitable inputs are rejected. Foraged instances with different nutrition/metadata may be rejected.

[h2]Configuration[/h2]

Server sandbox settings control fruit drying (enabled by default), drying hours (48), dried shelf-life multiplier (6), jams/jellies (enabled), sealed fresh days (30), action time multiplier (1) and jelly fruit yield (70%). Existing batches/jars retain their accepted food settings. Food inspection and the read-only apple/rack preview have separate toggles.

Marmalade, fruit butter, pickling, curing and fermentation remain planned, not implemented. This test upload does not establish MP correctness or a stable release.
